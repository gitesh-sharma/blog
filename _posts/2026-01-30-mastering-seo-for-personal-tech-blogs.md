Search Engine Optimization (SEO) isn't just for massive content portals; it's essential for getting your personal daily blog discovered by software engineers and tech enthusiasts.

### Key SEO Checklist for Daily Bloggers:

1. **Structured Schema Markup (`BlogPosting`)**
   Embedding Schema.org JSON-LD metadata helps Googlebot and Bingbot understand the publish date, author, headline, and article body.

2. **Clean Permalinks & Canonical URLs**
   Use readable, keyword-rich permalink structures like `/:title/` and specify canonical tags to prevent duplicate content issues.

3. **Optimized Images & WebP Format**
   Compress blog cover images using modern formats like `.webp` and always include descriptive `alt` tags for web accessibility and image search indexing.

4. **Internal Linking**
   Link previous daily posts inside new articles to distribute link equity and increase session duration.

Building an SEO-friendly daily blog ensures that every article you publish continues to drive organic traffic long after publication.
